Linux

Linux is a family of open-source Unix-like operating systems based on the Linux kernel, first released on September 17, 1991 by Linus Torvalds.

It powers over 96% of the world's top web servers, 100% of the top 500 supercomputers, and billions of Android devices worldwide.

🐧 About Linux

Linux was created by Linus Torvalds in 1991 as a free and open-source alternative to proprietary UNIX systems.

Today it powers everything from smartphones to the world's fastest supercomputers, embedded devices, and cloud infrastructure.
