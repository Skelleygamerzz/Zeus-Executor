Linux
Linux is a family of open-source Unix-like operating systems based on the Linux kernel, first released on September 17, 1991 by Linus Torvalds.
It powers everything from smartphones (Android) to the world's top supercomputers, web servers, and now — LunarOS mobile devices.

🐧 About Linux
Linux was created by Linus Torvalds as a free and open-source alternative to MINIX. The first version (0.01) was released in 1991.
Today, Linux powers over 96% of the world's top web servers, 100% of the top 500 supercomputers, and billions of Android devices worldwide.
